1. Executive Summary & Vision

1.1 Problem Statement: Modern dental practices operate under extreme sensory, ergonomic, and cognitive demands. Existing dental software suites (legacy on-premise systems like Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or generic cloud PMS) suffer from cluttered desktop interfaces with tiny touch targets unsuitable for gloved chairside operatory use, severe visual wash-out under intense operatory surgical lighting (5,000–10,000 Lux), disconnected workflows between patient reception, clinical charting, digital diagnostics/radiology, and patient billing, and fragile communication channels with patients (unreliable SMS vs. modern structured WhatsApp booking).

1.2 Product Vision: Operatory Pro is a high-precision, tactile, cloud-native dental operatory and practice management workstation. Engineered with ergonomic chairside workflows, zero-latency clinical state caching, high-contrast surgical visibility (WCAG AAA), and integrated bilingual localization (English / Arabic RTL / French), Operatory Pro unifies reception, clinical diagnostics, treatment planning, and chairside financial settlement into a single, cohesive experience.

3. Product Architecture & Synchronized Environments

1. Administrative Suite (Front-of-House)

  • Main Dashboard & Day Schedule: Multi-chair utilization timeline, filter by doctor.
  • WhatsApp Inbound Booking Triage: Human-in-the-loop review queue and identity matching.
  • Prescriptions & Invoicing: A4/A5 PDF generation, instant dispatch, claims tracking.
  • Financial Collections & Ledger: Clinic totals, revenue breakdown, fee adjustments.
  • Practice & Staff Settings: Working hours, provider operatory assignments, credentials.

2. Operatory Workstation (Chairside)

  • Patient Clinical Dossier Header: Persistent MRN, critical allergy banner (Penicillin/Latex).
  • 3D/2D Odontogram: Universal 1–32 and FDI tooth notation, condition tagging (Crown, Endo, Sound).
  • Radiographic DICOM Viewer: Panoramic OPG, bitewings, calipers, AI pathology triage tags.
  • Multi-Stage Treatment Roadmap: Phased plan stepper with patient digital consent capture.
  • Chairside Terminal: One-touch copay collection (Card, Apple Pay, Cash, HSA/FSA).

4. Detailed Feature & Functional Specifications

4.1 Daily Operatory Schedule & Day Matrix (U02, SCREEN_24)

Proportional time grid (08:30–18:00) with 15-minute cadence ticks and hour demarcation lines. Appointment cards scale directly to procedure duration (15m, 30m, 45m, 60m, 90m) without rounding up. Side-by-side multi-chair columns for Operatory 1 (Dr. Jenkins) and Operatory 2 (Dr. Alami). Real-time chair utilization metric (e.g., 87% Active), completed vs. in-chair patient tallies, and daily production revenue counters.

4.2 Inbound WhatsApp Booking & Human-in-the-Loop Triage (FRONTEND_UX.md)

Patient initiates conversational request. Inbound requests land in a shared clinic queue. Receptionists or attending dentists review request details, verify identity (or register new patient without losing draft), adjust duration/time, and approve. Mutual broadcast sends simultaneous confirmations to both patient and requester. Database exclusion constraints strictly prevent double-booking across multiple chairs.

4.3 Patient Clinical Overview & Records (SCREEN_2, SCREEN_16)

Persistent dossier banner with patient MRN (#P6L272), verified active status, attending doctor, primary insurance benefit status, emergency contact, and critical allergy pill flags. Real-time vitals and perio risk strip (BP 118/76 mmHg, pulse/SpO2 waveform, plaque index 18%, bleeding on probing 14%, pocket depths >4mm). Structured SOAP documentation module with doctor signature stamp.

4.4 Dental Imaging & DICOM Radiographic Diagnostics (SCREEN_6)

Diagnostic series reel supporting Panoramic Full Arch (OPG 2D), Bitewing Left/Right, Periapical (PA), and CBCT 3D volume selector. DICOM viewport micro-tools: Window/Level (W/L) contrast adjustment slider, digital caliper measurement tool (e.g., 14.6 mm Alveolar Crest), inverted tone mode, zoom loupe, and angle measurement. AI diagnostic pathology triage overlay flagging caries demineralization depth (Tooth #15 Interproximal 94% Conf.) and periapical radiolucencies.

4.5 Multi-Stage Treatment Planning & Chairside Ledger (SCREEN_4, SCREEN_10)

Phased treatment stepper: Urgent/Pain Relief (Completed) → Restorative & Endo (In-Progress) → Crown & Bridge (Scheduled) → Perio Maintenance (Pending). CDT procedure matrix with pre-auth approval badges, fee schedules, insurance allowable, and patient copay. Chairside ledger with real-time split-payment calculation, Delta Dental PPO 67.1% coverage visualization, and one-touch payment collector supporting Credit Card, Apple Pay, Cash, and HSA/FSA debit accounts.

  • Primary Action: #192F86 (Deep Royal Cobalt)
  • Critical Contraindication: #B42318 on #FEF3F2
  • Completed / Adjudicated: #176544 on #ECFDF3

Ergonomics & Typography

  • Minimum Hit Target: 44px × 44px (Operatory gloved touch standard)
  • Corner Radii Hierarchy: 10px (controls), 12px (chips), 16px (cards), 24px (containers)
  • Latin Font: Inter Variable (400, 500, 600, 700)
  • Arabic / RTL: Alyamama / Tahoma
  • Tabular Figures: Mandatory tnum for all fees, CDT codes, and coordinates

6. Non-Functional & Technical Verification Matrix

Dimension Specification Verification Metric
Form Factor Primary: Desktop/Laptop (1440px / 1920px); Secondary: Chairside Tablet (1024px landscape). Zero horizontal page scroll; fluid responsive columns down to 1024px.
Performance SPA/SSR architecture; initial page paint < 1.2s; tab switching < 50ms. Instantaneous DOM tab shifts via zero-latency state caching.
Accessibility Compliance with WCAG 2.1 AAA for clinical text and medical alert banners (contrast ratio ≥ 7:1). Automated Lighthouse Accessibility score ≥ 98.
Security & Privacy HIPAA certified, encrypted at rest and in transit (TLS 1.3), HttpOnly SameSite Strict session cookies. Zero plaintext PHI/PII leakage; explicit session expiration after 12h operatory lock.
Resilience Automatic revalidation on reconnect with preserved form drafts; idempotent mutation IDs (request-id). Zero duplicated payments or appointments on flaky clinic Wi-Fi.
